### About
I work on **computer vision and deep learning for real-world imaging**: medical (blood-cell) images,
agricultural quality inspection and illumination-robust recognition. I also have a background in
**power electronics**, including active power filters and harmonics. Every project here comes with
runnable **Python and MATLAB** code.

### Research interests
`Computer vision` · `Deep learning` · `Medical image analysis` · `Image enhancement` ·
`Biometrics` · `Power quality & active filters`

### Open-science principles
Each paper ships with code and a one-command reproduction script. Results are reported as
mean ± std over at least 3 seeds, including negative results. Releases are archived with a DOI.
